Who we are

Women for Women Rwanda is a community-driven organization that empowers vulnerable and marginalized women across Rwanda. We combine social support, economic training, and leadership development to help women regain dignity and achieve independence.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Mission+
We are a national organization that aims to empower women and girls in Rwanda to break the cycle of poverty and social exclusion, promoting better health, education and social conditions.
Vision+
We envision a society where women and girls are socio-economically empowered to enable communities for sustainable development.
Core Values+
Integrity

Honesty and ethical conduct.

Teamwork

Delivering as one team.

Respect

Mutual respect for diversity.

Accountability

Taking responsibility for impact.

Dignity

Representing with dignity.

Excellence

Delivering high quality work.

Programs & Impact

Core 12-month Program

Four modules: health & wellbeing, economic empowerment, rights & leadership, and business skills. Includes VSLA formation, stipends and mentorship support.

Who it serves: Vulnerable women, survivors and young mothers in rural and semi-urban areas.

Entrepreneurship Skills Program

Short practical trainings in tailoring, food processing and agribusiness. Graduates receive mentorship and market access support.

Market Linkages

We connect graduates with buyers, support product quality, packaging and business coaching to scale income opportunities.

Program Highlights

  • 12-month core curriculum
  • VSLA setup and digitization
  • Access to mentorship and markets
